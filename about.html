Our Vision & Purpose

Dignified Livelihoods for Daily-Wage Workers

SkillConnect replaces the uncertain physical “Labour Chowk” with a trusted digital platform that empowers workers with fair daily wages, verified safety, and immediate local matching.

⚠️

The Traditional Labor Chowk

  • • Workers must stand on crowded street corners starting at 5:30 AM with no work guarantee.
  • • Middlemen (Thekedars) frequently deduct 25% to 40% of their earned daily wages.
  • • Wage disputes, delayed payments, and unverified work safety.
  • • Contractors struggle to find skilled, reliable kaarigars when needed urgently.
✨

The SkillConnect Solution

  • • Direct job alerts sent via SMS/WhatsApp within 5–10 km of the worker’s home.
  • • 100% Zero Commission deducted from the laborer’s daily income.
  • • Aadhaar authentication & transparent government-standard wage guidelines.
  • • Fast 1-click hire for contractors, site supervisors, and residential homeowners.

Our Core Values

Guiding principles built into every feature of the SkillConnect portal.

🛡️

Dignity of Labor

Treating every trade—from helper to master mason—with respect, fair wages, and recognition.

📍

Hyperlocal First

Prioritizing work within walking or short bicycling distance so workers save on commute costs.

🤝

Community Trust

Building local peer ratings, contractor reviews, and verified profile credentials.
